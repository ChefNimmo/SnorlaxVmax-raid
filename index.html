Raid history

Snorlax Raid Rules · v2.1

Setup & difficulty

Choose 1–6 players and one of eight difficulty levels. The boss deck is always exactly 60 cards. Difficulty can adjust the universal Trainer package, the automatic minion draw interval X, Standard versus Nightmare minion deck, and Snorlax-specific modifiers. This app also scales damage per life card by player count and difficulty. The suggested X intervals for levels 1–8 are 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3 boss-deck draws respectively; they are configurable encounter defaults, not official Pokémon rules.

Universal boss deck · exactly 60 cards

Shuffle these four components into one boss deck. Stadiums are separate from the 12 signature Trainers:

The 26-card universal package can change with difficulty. Snorlax gets its own 12-card Trainer and four-card Stadium packages. Colress’s Experiment ×1 is included in the signature Trainer allocation.

Separate Standard and Nightmare minion decks

Standard uses 60 Pokémon cards; Nightmare uses a concentrated 20-card Pokémon deck of dangerous minions. Neither is shuffled into the 60-card boss deck. The five-slot Bench and staging rules apply in both modes.

Exactly when automatic minions deploy

Track cards drawn from the boss deck, including ordinary boss draws and additional draw effects. After every X such cards drawn, immediately trigger one draw from the separate minion deck. If a single effect crosses multiple X thresholds, trigger one minion per threshold. The trigger happens as soon as the draw count reaches X, before continuing with later unrelated effects. The app announces triggers; physically draw the minion, then use Add Minion to record it. Search effects are an independent source of minions and do not increment the automatic draw counter. Searching for a card, discarding, revealing without drawing, and putting cards in the Lost Zone do not themselves count as drawing. Drawn minions enter the Bench if a slot is open; otherwise use difficulty-based shuffle-back or staging.

Boss turn & G-Max Fall

Snorlax stays Active. Attach Energy to Snorlax until it has three Colourless Energy, then to minions unless printed effects say otherwise. G-Max Fall deals 60 + 30 per occupied minion slot to all players. Resolve printed effects and Energy physically.

Minion searches and staging

Five minion slots. Minions count as Active and Benched. Searches into hand instead deploy eligible Pokémon directly; printed Basic/HP and other restrictions apply. Full Bench: levels 1–4 return searched Pokémon to the minion deck and shuffle; 5–6 stage up to two; 7–8 stage unlimited. Stage 1/2 deploy directly when eligible. Stage first-in-first-out on confirmed KO. Direct deployment does not trigger effects requiring playing from hand.

Sleep and special conditions

Resolve printed attacks, Abilities, Sleep checks and restrictions at the table. Hypno Blinding Aura and Sleep Inducer follow their printed text.

Healing & Food Reserve

Fixed-value printed healing may heal a minion or add that amount to pending Food Reserve. Only actual healing triggers on-heal effects. Pending reserve activates next round and absorbs damage. Max Potion heals all damage from one minion, requires discarding all attached Energy physically, and cannot create Food Reserve.

Boss life, damage & Lost Zone

Enter each attack damage and minion allocation. Remaining boss damage, after active protection, accumulates over all players. Resolve once at end of round: floor(total damage ÷ damage per life card), plus explicit Lost Zone modifier. Discard costs (e.g. Ultra Ball) are physical discards and do not reduce life. Cards sent explicitly to Lost Zone do.

Colress’s Experiment

Include one in the unique Trainer package. Raid adaptation: reveal five Trainer/Energy cards, choose three to resolve in order, put two in Lost Zone. Add +2 to the round modifier or apply immediately, not both. Respect other printed costs and one-Supporter-per-turn limit.

Minion HP, Cape & KO

Edit printed HP and other HP modifiers. Hero’s Cape adds 100 HP. When removing Cape leaves current HP at zero or below, show KO pending; never auto-remove. Confirm KO to free the slot and deploy the next staged minion.
